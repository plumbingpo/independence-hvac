Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Saint Louis

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners last 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

Minor issues don't stay minor when they're ignored. Take a weak capacitor: replacing it is a quick, inexpensive repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to keep our number in their phone and reach out at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a few miserable nights.
