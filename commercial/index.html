Kitchen Climate Control

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

In a restaurant, hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort are all connected, and a system that's undersized or failing reveals itself as a sweltering cook line and complaints at the tables. Cooking lines pour enormous heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air out as fast as the rooftop units can make it. We handle rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Many restaurant comfort complaints trace back to air balance. When a kitchen runs negative, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, sucks hot or cold outdoor air through every door, and wears out your staff at peak service. We balance the makeup air to the hood exhaust and document every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Independence HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Regular professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming liabilities. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Independence

An HVAC emergency on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, empty a retail floor on a summer afternoon, overheat a server room, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Independence HVAC provides fast, emergency HVAC service for commercial properties across Independence and surrounding areas. When you call our commercial line, we dispatch an experienced technician quickly, because every hour without climate control costs you money.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: our technicians have seen them all on rooftop and split systems. Preventive Care

Scheduled HVAC Maintenance for Commercial Properties

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment is preventing failures before they happen. Independence HVAC offers customized scheduled maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, coil cleaning and condensate line clearing, filter changes with MERV ratings matched to the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant charge and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal preparation for peak demand throughout the year. Each program is tailored to the property's specific equipment, usage patterns, and budget requirements.

Compared with an emergency replacement, scheduled maintenance costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints.
